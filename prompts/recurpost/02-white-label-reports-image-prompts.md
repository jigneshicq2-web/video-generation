# RecurPost Meta Ads: White-Label Reports
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** the only facts used are the ones confirmed in public sources: reports carry the **agency's own logo/branding**, can be **scheduled for automatic recurring delivery**, and pull **performance data from multiple platforms into one report**. Do NOT let the image model invent features like "custom domain", "client portal", "AI insights" or specific % uplifts. Check the live product before adding anything else.

**Important visual rule for this feature:** the report shown inside each ad must be branded with a **fictional agency** (e.g. "Pixel & Co. Agency"), NOT with RecurPost. That's the whole point of white-label: the client never sees RecurPost. RecurPost's logo appears only once, in the ad's own logo position.
