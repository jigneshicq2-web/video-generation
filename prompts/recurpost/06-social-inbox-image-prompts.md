# RecurPost Meta Ads: Unified Social Inbox (+ AI-drafted replies)
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** only these facts are used, as confirmed in public sources and RecurPost's knowledge base:
1. **One unified inbox** for messages, comments and reviews across connected platforms, including **Instagram Business, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and Google Business Profile**. One review site says "9 platforms"; that count isn't used in any ad until it's verified.
2. **AI-drafted replies.**

Don't claim response-time improvements, sentiment analysis, auto-send without review, or any SLA figure. In every ad, AI replies are shown as a **draft the human reviews/sends**.

**Visual rule:** inbox messages come from fictional customers of a fictional client ("Bloom Café"). RecurPost's logo appears only once.

---

Mix: 4 Hinglish / 6 English · 6 light / 4 dark · 8 distinct visual formats.
