# RecurPost Meta Ads: Bulk Scheduling + AI Content (CSV upload, AI captions & images, best time to post, Canva)
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** only these facts are used, as confirmed in public sources:
1. **Bulk scheduling via CSV upload**
2. **AI caption/content assistant** and **AI image generator** (reportedly on the Agency plan, "10+ AI features")
3. **AI "best time to post"**
4. **Canva integration**
5. Agency plan allows **up to 80 posts per profile per day** (a limit, not a recommendation; don't imply anyone should post that much)

Don't claim specific time savings, engagement uplift from best-time posting, or that AI writes "in your brand voice" unless verified. Example figures used in the ads are labelled as examples.
