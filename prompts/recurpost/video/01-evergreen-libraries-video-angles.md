# RecurPost Video Ads: Feature 1, Evergreen Content Libraries
## 10 angles for 30–40s motion-graphics ads (9:16, sound-off first)

**Targets:** hook rate ≥30% (3-sec plays ÷ impressions), hold rate ≥20% (ThruPlays ÷ 3-sec plays).
**Audience:** agency owners and digital marketers who manage multiple client accounts.

---

## Why the past AI videos stalled at 12% (and the rules these follow)

1. **Motion by frame 6 (0.2s).** No fade-ins, no logo intro, no slow push. The first frame must already be mid-action.
2. **Frame 1 works as a thumbnail.** Bold text plus one image that means something even if the video is paused.
3. **Callout or tension in the first 1.5s.** Name the viewer ("Agency owners:") or show a number or scene that hurts.
4. **Sound-off first.** Every spoken line is also on screen, burned in, in the upper-middle third (inside the Reels safe zone).
5. **Pattern interrupt every 2–3s.** A cut, zoom, number slam, whip or colour flip. Never more than 3s of static frame.
6. **An open loop before 5s** ("…by the end of this, you'll never…", or a counter that hasn't finished). This drives hold rate.
7. **Logo and product name only after the reveal (~12–18s),** never in the hook.
8. **No generic AI look:** no glowing brains, robots or stock handshakes. Show recognisable agency artefacts: content calendars, client WhatsApp messages, Canva tabs, post cards, analytics.

## Common structure (30–40s)

| Time | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **HOOK** | Stop the scroll: callout, number or visual shock |
| 3–8s | **AGITATE** | Make the pain specific and personal |
| 8–14s | **TURN** | The insight: "the problem isn't X, it's Y" |
| 14–26s | **MECHANISM** | Product motion: libraries → categories → auto-repeat (animated UI) |
| 26–33s | **PAYOFF** | The outcome they feel (time, calm, scale), shown visually |
| 33–38s | **CTA** | Logo + one action + end card |

## Beat outlines

### 1. The Content Graveyard (36s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** Post cards rain down in a dark scene and pile into a grave mound. A headstone slams in: "Your best post. Seen once. Then buried." *VO: "Your best post got one day of attention."*
- **3–8 AGITATE:** A counter flies up, "1… 50… 400… 1,000+ posts", as more cards bury the first one. Text: "Every month you make new ones. The winners? Gone." *VO: "Then you buried it under 600 more."*
- **8–14 TURN:** Freeze. One card glows through the pile. Text: "Good content doesn't expire. Your system just forgets it."
- **14–26 MECHANISM:** The glowing card lifts out and drops into a **Library** box (RecurPost UI). Category tabs pop in: Tips / Testimonials / Offers. A loop arrow (the logo's clock arrow) spins and the card republishes onto a calendar on Mon, Wed and Fri, week after week. RecurPost logo lands here.
- **26–33 PAYOFF:** The graveyard reverses: the pile flies back up into neat libraries, and the calendar fills itself. Text: "Your best work, working on repeat."
- **33–36 CTA:** End card: RecurPost logo + "Build your first evergreen library" + button "Start Free Trial."

### 2. Day 1. Again. (34s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A colourful full month calendar → hard SWOOSH wipe to blank → big stamp "Day 1. Again."
- **3–8 AGITATE:** Quick cuts: the 1st of the month × 3 (Jan, Feb, Mar), each wiped blank. Text: "20 clients. Blank calendars. Every month."
- **8–14 TURN:** Text: "What if the calendar didn't reset… because the content never left?"
- **14–26 MECHANISM:** Libraries drop in as stacked colour blocks; category schedules ("Tips → Tue", "Testimonials → Fri") draw lines into the calendar; it fills automatically and keeps filling into next month as it scrolls. Logo.
- **26–31 PAYOFF:** The 1st of the next month arrives: the calendar is already full. Stamp: "Day 1. Done."
- **31–34 CTA.**

### 3. The 600 Problem (35s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** "20 clients" SLAM, "× 30 posts" SLAM, "= 600" SLAM, with camera shake. "Every. Single. Month."
- **3–8 AGITATE:** The 600 explodes into 600 tiny post tiles filling the screen. Text: "Written. Designed. Scheduled. By hand."
- **8–14 TURN:** Tiles sort by colour. Text: "But most of these are the same 5 types of post…"
- **14–26 MECHANISM:** Those 5 types become 5 libraries. A recycling loop runs. The equation rewrites: "600 to create" becomes "create once, repeat on schedule."
- **26–32 PAYOFF:** A big clock shows hours freed (no specific claim; visual only). Text: "Spend the time on strategy, not scheduling."
- **32–35 CTA.**
- ⚠️ Keep "600" framed as example maths (20 × 30), not a customer claim.

### 4. The Callout (32s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** Hard cut, yellow background, black type punching in word by word: "Agency owners: stop scrolling if you've written a 'Monday Motivation' post more than 10 times."
- **3–9 AGITATE:** A checklist ticks: ☑ "Rewritten the same tip 5 ways" ☑ "Rescheduled last year's festive posts" ☑ "Copied a post between 6 clients". Text: "Yeah. Us too."
- **9–14 TURN:** "You don't need new content. You need content that comes back."
- **14–25 MECHANISM:** Library UI animation, categories, auto-repeat toggle flips ON.
- **25–29 PAYOFF:** "Write it once. RecurPost keeps posting it."
- **29–32 CTA.**

### 5. Sunday 11:47 PM (36s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** An analog clock whips to SUN 11:47 PM; a dark room; laptop glow; a phone buzzes with the notification "Client: Posts for this week?" Text: "If this is your Sunday, watch 30 seconds."
- **3–9 AGITATE:** Quick montage in motion graphics: Canva tab → caption doc → scheduler → next client → repeat, accelerating. Text: "Client 7 of 20…"
- **9–14 TURN:** Everything stops. "What if this week was already scheduled… from last month?"
- **14–27 MECHANISM:** Libraries + category schedules + auto-repeat; the week fills for all 20 client workspaces at once (a grid of 20 mini calendars filling).
- **27–33 PAYOFF:** The clock rewinds to SUN 11:47 AM: sunlight, a closed laptop, chai. Text: "Get your Sundays back."
- **33–36 CTA.**

### 6. The Content Treadmill (33s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A post icon runs on a hamster wheel spinning faster; text: "You're not growing your agency. You're refilling it."
- **3–8 AGITATE:** The wheel speeds up; little post cards fly off and vanish. Text: "Run faster → make more → it disappears → repeat."
- **8–13 TURN:** The wheel cracks. "Stop feeding the wheel. Build a library."
- **13–25 MECHANISM:** The wheel morphs into the logo's circular arrow and becomes the recycling engine: posts go in once and keep coming out on schedule.
- **25–30 PAYOFF:** "Same effort. Compounding output."
- **30–33 CTA.**

### 7. The Expiry Date (34s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A post card slides in; a red milk-carton stamp THUDS: "EXPIRES: 24 HRS." Text: "Why does your content have an expiry date?"
- **3–8 AGITATE:** More cards get stamped and drop into a bin. Text: "Hours of design. A day of life."
- **8–13 TURN:** "Evergreen content doesn't expire. Treat it that way."
- **13–25 MECHANISM:** The stamp peels off and becomes a yellow "EVERGREEN ∞" sticker; the card goes into a library and auto-repeats on schedule.
- **25–31 PAYOFF:** The bin empties back out into a living calendar.
- **31–34 CTA.**

### 8. The Myth (36s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A speech bubble: "Reposting old content annoys followers." A red slash, "MYTH."
- **3–9 AGITATE / REFRAME:** An animated feed scrolls fast; one post flashes by and is missed. Text: "Most of your followers never saw it the first time."
- **9–14 TURN:** "Your best posts deserve a second, third, tenth chance, at the right spacing."
- **14–28 MECHANISM:** Library → category → repeat interval (e.g. "every 6 weeks") shown on a timeline; different posts rotate so the feed stays varied.
- **28–33 PAYOFF:** "Fresh-looking feed. Recycled engine."
- **33–36 CTA.**
- ⚠️ "Most followers never saw it" is a general organic-reach truth. Don't attach a % unless you have a sourced figure.

### 9. Same Agency, Two Months (35s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A split screen snaps in. LEFT: the frantic manager, 14 tabs, coffee spilling. RIGHT: the same person sipping chai, calm. Text: "Same agency. Same 20 clients. One difference."
- **3–9 AGITATE:** The left side intensifies (tabs multiply, a WhatsApp ping storm); the right side stays calm. Viewer waits for the reveal.
- **9–14 TURN:** A zoom into the right screen: "The difference?"
- **14–27 MECHANISM:** The right screen shows the RecurPost libraries filling 20 client calendars on autopilot.
- **27–32 PAYOFF:** The left side fades and only calm remains. "Pick your side."
- **32–35 CTA.**

### 10. Phir Se? (Hinglish, 34s)
- **0–3 HOOK:** A caption in a doc gets typed fast, deleted, retyped, deleted. Text: "Har mahine wahi content… phir se likhoge?" Counter: "Rewrite #47."
- **3–8 AGITATE:** "Diwali post. Phir se. Testimonial post. Phir se. Tip of the week. Phir se." Each "phir se" slams.
- **8–13 TURN:** "Ek baar likho. Baar baar chalao."
- **13–25 MECHANISM:** Libraries + category schedules + auto-repeat (UI motion, Hinglish captions).
- **25–31 PAYOFF:** The rewrite counter freezes, then resets to "Rewrite #0". "Ab likhna kam, badhna zyada."
- **31–34 CTA:** "Free trial shuru karo."

## Which to produce first

| Priority | Angles | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1st wave | **4 (Callout), 1 (Graveyard), 3 (600), 5 (Sunday)** | Strongest first-1.5s mechanics: identity callout, visual shock, number slam, relatable scene |
| 2nd wave | 7, 8, 9 | Strong but rely on curiosity, which is slightly slower to land |
| Market test | 10 | Run against the English version in India only |
| Lower | 2, 6 | Good metaphors, weaker first frame; keep as alternates |

**Also cut a 15s version** of each first-wave angle (hook + turn + mechanism flash + CTA). If the 35s versions miss the hold target, this shows whether the problem is the hook or the middle.
