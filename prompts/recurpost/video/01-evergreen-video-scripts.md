# RecurPost Video Ads: Feature 1, Evergreen Content Libraries
## 10 production scripts (EV-01 → EV-10), 30–40s, 9:16, motion graphics

**Read `00-video-master-brief.md` first.** Everything here assumes its brand, type, motion, audio, CTA end-card and QA rules.

**How to read each script:**
- Times are real seconds at 30 fps. The music is 120 BPM, so 1 beat = 0.5s; beats are numbered from 0.
- **TEXT** lines are exact on-screen copy (quoted). [Y] = yellow highlight treatment per brand rules.
- **VO** lines are exact TTS input, with start time.
- **SFX** cues use the master SFX names.
- **Frame 0 / thumbnail** is specified for every ad; build it first and check it as a still.

**Build order:** EV-04 → EV-01 → EV-03 → EV-05 (first wave), then EV-07, EV-08, EV-09, EV-10, EV-02, EV-06.
