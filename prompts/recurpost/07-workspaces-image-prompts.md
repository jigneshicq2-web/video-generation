# RecurPost Meta Ads: Workspaces (one workspace per client brand)
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** only these facts are used, as confirmed in public sources: **separate workspaces per client brand** for a multi-brand structure, and **role-based approval workflows**. **Per-workspace team access** ("this teammate sees only these clients") is used in V4 and V9 but isn't confirmed. Verify it before running those two, or swap to the fallback copy given.

**Visual rule:** client brands are fictional ("Bloom Café", "Apex Law Partners", "Kiko Toys", "UrbanFit Gym"), each shown with a simple abstract geometric mark only. No real brand logos. RecurPost's logo appears once.
