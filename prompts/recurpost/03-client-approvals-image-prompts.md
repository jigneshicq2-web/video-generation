# RecurPost Meta Ads: Client Approvals (approve by link, no client login)
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** only these facts are used, as confirmed in public sources:
1. The client approves posts via a **shareable link**, with **no login/account needed**.
2. There is an **internal team approval step** before content goes to the client.
3. **Shareable client calendars** exist.

The approval UI in these ads shows only an **"Approve"** action. Before adding "Request changes", comments, mobile-app approvals or notifications to any ad, check whether RecurPost has them. Don't let the image model invent them.

**Visual rule:** approval screens shown to the client carry a **fictional agency brand** ("Pixel & Co. Agency") and a fictional client brand ("Bloom Café"). RecurPost's logo appears only once, in the ad's own logo spot.
