# RecurPost Meta Ads: Client Connect (clients connect their own accounts, no passwords shared)
## 10 variations + AI image-generation prompts

---

## SETUP

Use `00-master-style-guide.md`: upload the right logo, paste **Block A** once, then paste the variation prompts below one at a time. Check every output against **Block C** in the same file.

**Claim guardrail:** only these facts are used, as confirmed in public sources: **password-free client onboarding**. The agency sends an **invite**, and the client **connects their own social profiles** themselves, so the agency never needs the client's passwords. Before adding "one-click", "2FA-safe", "revoke anytime" or any time figure to an ad, check whether RecurPost actually offers it.

**Visual rule:** invite screens show a **fictional agency** ("Pixel & Co. Agency") and a **fictional client** ("Bloom Café"). RecurPost's logo appears only once, in the ad's own logo spot. Never show a real-looking password anywhere; use dots.
